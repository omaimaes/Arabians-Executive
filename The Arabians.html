Director's Vision · Visual Motifs

Recurring Imagery

Four images we return to — each one carries meaning.
Motif 01

The Horse

→ The Legend

The prize everyone is chasing — shown glowing in backlight or slow motion, beautiful and almost mythic. When the horse appears, everything else in the frame falls away.

Motif 02

The Handler

→ The Fighter

Framed alone under the spotlight, with everything to prove. The weight of the whole moment rests on one set of shoulders — a single figure against the dark.

Motif 03

The Farm Owner

→ The Strategist

Shown in the act of making the decisions that change everything. Power read through stillness — every result radiates out from the choices made in the VIP seats.

Motif 04

The Stable

→ The Battlefield

Narrow and tense, with shadows closing in from every edge. This is where ambition and rivalry are fought out of sight, long before the arena lights ever come on.

Social Media · Before We Build

Key Commercial Considerations

Two dependencies that shape the scope of everything that follows.
01Dependency · Deal Terms

Scope is subject to distribution deal terms

Under Distribution Track 1 (Global Platform Acquisition), our ability to operate independent social channels will be restricted. Platforms typically retain control over content published alongside their acquisitions. Content scope, channel ownership, posting frequency and publishing rights must all be formally negotiated with the platform.

Negotiated per acquisition deal
02Dependency · Budget

Content & account management is not yet budgeted

The production of social content — score-reveal graphics, leaderboard assets, character cards, short-form edits and gap-event coverage — and the ongoing management of accounts across YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and the hosted hub, has not been included in the current production budgets.

Requires an incremental line

Social Media · The Content System

Four Content Types

Each with its own purpose and cadence.
01
Reach

Promos & Trailers

  • Series, character and story highlights — with a strong CTA to the platform
  • Short-form cuts optimised per platform: Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts
  • Arabic and English versions produced per positioning strategy
  • The only type published across all three account sets
02
World-building

The Ecosystem

  • Farm profiles, handler bios, scoring explainer, circuit map
  • Hosted on The Arabians microsite — linked from social
  • Introduced at launch and maintained throughout the run
  • 5 criteria · 16 judges · high & low scores removed
03
Engagement

The Shadow Circuit

  • Pre-drop: standings & gap results before each episode airs
  • Post-drop: score-reveal card 48–72h after — winner, top 3, movement
  • Designed shareable graphic + 15-sec motion cut for Reels & TikTok
  • Living leaderboard on the hub, updated every episode
04
Retention

Gap Events

  • Covers GCAT events not featured in any episode — exclusive content
  • Character check-ins, results and leaderboard shifts between episodes
  • Rewards the most engaged viewers with what the series doesn't show
  • Makes the full 14-event GCAT circuit feel complete
